    FAQ

    Good to know

    Which links work?

    Public video and reel links from Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn and Snapchat. Short links such as youtu.be, fb.watch and lnkd.in work too.

    Why did my link fail?

    The post is probably private, deleted, age-restricted or limited to logged-in viewers. Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Snapchat change often, so some public posts can fail until the server’s downloader is updated.

    What quality can I choose?

    Whatever the source offers. After you paste a link, the available resolutions appear as buttons, plus an audio-only option.

    Where does the file go?

    Straight to your browser’s downloads folder. The server deletes its temporary copy as soon as the file has been sent.

    Is it legal to download videos?

    Only save content you own or have permission to use. Respect creators’ rights and each platform’s terms. Lumora Save isn’t affiliated with any of these platforms.
